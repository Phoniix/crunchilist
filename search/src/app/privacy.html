Privacy

Crunchilist Search finds anime across streaming services you connect and keeps your AniList list in sync with what you watch.

What stays on this device

Watch history reads, remembered matches, import previews, checkpoints, catalogs and settings stay in your browser. They are never uploaded to our servers — we don't run a user-data server.

What leaves this device

  • AniList — when you connect AniList, sign-in and list updates use AniList's own API with your account.
  • GitHub raw feeds — the master title list and related public feeds are downloaded from this project's public repository.
  • Streaming sites you connect — history and catalog reads use your own signed-in session in a tab you open. Nothing is changed on those sites.

AI

This mockup does not use AI. Title search works against the master list and AniList without any AI provider.

Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
